Emergency Quick Access

If someone may be in danger, call 112 now.

CALL 112

Heart and collapse warning signs

  • Severe chest pain or pressure, especially spreading to the arm, jaw or back
  • Sudden collapse, fainting, or the person does not respond
  • No normal breathing, or only gasping (possible cardiac arrest)

Breathing warning signs

  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Cannot speak full sentences because of breathlessness
  • Blue or grey lips or face
  • Sudden worsening of breathing, including in asthma or COPD

Stroke warning signs

  • Face drooping on one side
  • Arm weakness or numbness
  • Slurred speech or sudden confusion

Bleeding

  • Bleeding that will not stop after firm pressure for several minutes

What to do now

  1. Call 112 immediately. Do not wait to fill in any form.
  2. Stay with the person and keep them calm.
  3. Apply firm pressure to any bleeding.
  4. If the person is not breathing normally, follow the instructions the emergency operator gives you.
  5. Do not give food or drink to an unconscious person.

This page does not diagnose any condition. It only shows warning signs and tells you to contact emergency services. If you are unsure, call 112. Not seeing a warning sign on this page does not mean the person is safe.
