Emergency Quick Access

If someone may be in danger, call 112 now.

CALL 112

Warning signs that need emergency help

  • Severe chest pain or pressure
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Sudden collapse or the person does not respond
  • Possible stroke signs: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech

What to do

  1. Call 112 immediately. Do not wait to fill in any form.
  2. Stay with the person and keep them calm.
  3. Do not give food or drink if the person is unconscious.
  4. If the person is not breathing normally, follow the instructions the emergency operator gives you.

This page does not diagnose any condition. It only shows warning signs and tells you to contact emergency services. If you are unsure, call 112.
